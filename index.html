Privacy Policy

How Voice Broadcast handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Voice Broadcast (the “App”) handles information. The App is designed to let nearby devices communicate by voice over a local Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot.

1. Information and permissions the App uses

  • Microphone: The App requests microphone access when you use voice broadcasting. Audio is captured to provide the live voice feature and is transmitted to connected devices according to the room connection. Do not broadcast private or sensitive conversations unless everyone involved agrees.
  • Camera: The App requests camera access to scan a room QR code when you choose to join by scanning.
  • Wi-Fi and network information: The App may access network information needed to establish or use a local connection, identify the room endpoint, and connect devices on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot.
  • Room connection details: A room QR code or room address may contain connection information, such as a local network address and a room token. Anyone who obtains valid room details may be able to attempt to connect. Share them only with intended participants.

2. How local voice communication works

The App is intended for local-network voice communication and does not require the App developer to operate a cloud voice server for this feature. Voice data is sent over the local network to devices connected to the room. The App developer does not intentionally receive or store voice recordings on a developer-operated server through this local voice feature.

Your device, network configuration, operating system, or other software may process technical information to make the connection work. Local-network communication is not a guarantee of end-to-end encryption. Use the App only on networks you trust.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information from the App or device, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction information, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from network information, as permitted by their services and your device settings. The information used and the purposes of processing can depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the ad configuration.

Google may use this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud, and provide related advertising services. For details, review Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The App developer does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Review their policies for details about their data handling.

4. How information is used

Information and permissions described above are used to:

  • provide voice broadcasting and listening between connected devices;
  • scan room QR codes and establish local connections;
  • display and measure advertisements through the advertising service; and
  • maintain app reliability, diagnose errors, and help prevent abuse or fraud where supported by the relevant service.

5. Storage and retention

The App is designed for live local voice communication rather than cloud recording. The developer does not intentionally store voice recordings on a developer-operated server through the local voice feature. Technical data may be temporarily processed on your device or by third-party services. Advertising and diagnostic data, if collected by third-party services, is retained according to those providers’ policies and settings.

6. Sharing of information

Voice data is sent to devices participating in the local room. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its partners as described above. The developer does not sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

7. Your choices and controls

  • You can deny or revoke microphone and camera permissions in your device settings, although the related features will not work without them.
  • You can stop broadcasting, leave a room, or close the room in the App.
  • You can manage advertising personalization and advertising identifiers through the controls available on your device and in Google’s services, where available.
  • You can stop using the App at any time.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not specifically designed to target children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a developer-operated account system. Advertising services may have their own rules and requirements relating to children. If you believe information has been handled inappropriately, contact the developer using the details below.

9. Security

The developer takes reasonable steps within the App’s design to limit unnecessary data handling. However, no wireless transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure. Protect your device and hotspot, use a strong hotspot password, and share room QR codes or tokens only with intended participants.

10. International use and third-party processing

Third-party providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Their processing is governed by their own terms, policies, and applicable safeguards.
