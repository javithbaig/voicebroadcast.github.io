PennyLeaf

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy describes how the PennyLeaf mobile application (“PennyLeaf,” “we,” or “the app”) handles information. It reflects the current app implementation. Please read it together with the privacy information provided by Google and your device’s operating system.

1. Information you enter

PennyLeaf does not require an account. The app stores the information you enter in its local app storage on your device, including:

  • Expense and income amounts, dates, currencies, categories, and notes.
  • Bill and renewal titles, categories, amounts, currencies, due dates, recurrence settings, reminder choices, and payment history.
  • Your selected currency and small app preference values.

PennyLeaf does not send these records to a PennyLeaf-operated server. The app does not connect to bank accounts or initiate bill payments. A payment you record is a bookkeeping entry only.

2. Notifications and time zone

If you enable notifications, PennyLeaf schedules bill and renewal reminders locally on your device. Notification permission is optional; reminders may not appear if permission is denied or device settings prevent delivery. The app reads the device’s current time zone to schedule reminders at the intended local time. Reminder content can include the reminder title and due-date timing.

3. Advertising and third-party services

PennyLeaf uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request banner, native, and occasional interstitial ads. The SDK and Google advertising services may collect or receive information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, diagnostic information, and ad delivery or performance data. IP address may be used to estimate general location. Google and its advertising partners process this information under their own policies and settings. PennyLeaf does not receive your locally stored transaction database through the ad SDK.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

You can review advertising and privacy controls provided by Google and your device. The current app build does not include a dedicated Google UMP consent and privacy-options flow; required consent notices and controls must be configured before distributing in regions where they are required.

4. CSV exports

When you export transactions, PennyLeaf creates a CSV file containing transaction date, type, amount, currency, category, and note in temporary app storage, then opens the device share sheet. The file is sent only to a destination you choose. That destination’s provider may store or process a copy under its own terms. Deleting records in PennyLeaf does not delete copies already shared or saved elsewhere. Temporary files are subject to cleanup by the operating system.

5. Purchases

The current build contains a demo “Remove ads” flow that can check Google Play Billing availability and look up a product. It does not complete a purchase or collect payment card details. If purchases are enabled in a future version, Google Play or the applicable app store will process the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy, and this policy will be updated to describe the live flow.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Your financial records are stored in the app’s local database and are protected by the device and operating system. PennyLeaf does not currently add a separate password or app-level encryption layer to that database. Keep your device secured with its built-in screen lock. Depending on your device and account settings, the operating system may back up app data; PennyLeaf does not control those backups.

Records remain on the device until you delete them, clear app storage, or remove the app, subject to any device backup or copies you have exported. You can:

  • Delete an individual transaction from the app.
  • Use Settings → Delete all transactions to delete transaction entries. This does not delete bill reminders or their separate payment history.
  • Delete an individual bill reminder to delete that reminder and its bill-payment history. Expense transactions created when those payments were recorded remain in transaction history until separately deleted.
  • Remove the app or clear its app storage using your device settings. Device backups and exported or shared copies may remain outside the app.

Because PennyLeaf has no user account and does not receive your financial records on a developer server, we cannot retrieve or remotely delete those local records for you.

7. Children’s privacy

PennyLeaf is a general personal-finance utility and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children through a PennyLeaf account or server. Advertising services may apply their own age and privacy rules. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us using the address below.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published with PennyLeaf and linked from the app’s store listing.
